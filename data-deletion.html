Delete your Lumiriver data

App: Lumiriver · Developer: Techmely

Lumiriver does not have user accounts, so there is no account to delete. Your data lives in two places: on your device, and with our analytics and advertising partners, linked only to device identifiers.

1. Delete game data on your device

  1. Uninstall Lumiriver from your device.
  2. This permanently deletes your progress, coins, boosters, settings and age-check answer. It cannot be undone.
  3. Purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Pack) stay with your Apple or Google account and can be restored if you reinstall.

2. Reset the identifiers our partners use

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID).
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn tracking off for Lumiriver. Uninstalling the App also resets the identifiers our analytics partners use.

3. Ask us to delete partner data

To ask us to request deletion of analytics and attribution data held by our partners (Google AdMob, Google Firebase, GameAnalytics, AppsFlyer):

  1. Email techmely.creation@gmail.com with the subject “Lumiriver data deletion request”.
  2. Include your device type (iPhone / Android) and, if you can, your advertising ID (Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Without an identifier we cannot match data to you, because we never collect your name or email in the game.
  3. We confirm your request within 7 days and complete it within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept
